Album

Osiris

The album explores grief, trauma, anger, isolation, and the struggle to keep going. Its songs move through darkness and emotional collapse toward healing, self-protection, and cautious hope. Overall, it’s about surviving loss without pretending the pain simply disappears.

Shock

The song explores the numbness and disorientation of psychological shock, using images of collapsing floors and free fall to show reality slipping away. It also portrays fate as an overpowering force that leaves the speaker feeling trapped, helpless, and emotionally disconnected.
Recorded in the 2010s, finished 2022.

Forsaken

Forsaken

“Forsaken” follows Pat as he returns to an empty home after the loss of his wife. Once-familiar surroundings become painful reminders of her absence, turning moments of apparent peace into waves of grief. Despite his attempts to suppress his emotions, memories of her continue to resurface, leaving him trapped between the comfort of the past and the unbearable reality of the present.

At its heart, the song captures a particularly haunting experience of grief: the instinctive expectation that someone who is gone will return. Out of habit, his mind recreates the familiar sounds of his wife coming home, briefly convincing him that she is there. But when he turns to look, he is met with nothing but silence. In that moment, the painful realization slowly sets in once again: she is never coming home.

“Forsaken” explores the cruel disconnect between what the heart still expects and what the mind knows to be true, portraying grief not as a single moment of loss, but as a reality one is forced to rediscover over and over again.
